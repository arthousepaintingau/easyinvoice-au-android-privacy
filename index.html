Privacy Policy — EasyInvoice AU for Android

Effective date: 21 November 2026

Art House Painting Pty Ltd ("we", "us", or "our") publishes EasyInvoice AU for Android ("the App"). The App is the Android application with package name com.appnextra.easyinvoiceau, distributed on Google Play.

This Privacy Policy applies only to that Android application. It does not apply to any other application, website, or product.

EasyInvoice AU is an invoicing tool. You can store a business profile, create invoices, save PDF copies on your device, and share those PDFs yourself. The App does not require an account.

1. Information stored on your device

Information you enter is stored in the App's private storage on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your invoices, customers, or business records, and we do not have access to those records.

Depending on what you enter, that on-device information can include:

  • Business name, Australian Business Number (ABN), email address, and phone number;
  • A logo image you choose with the Android photo picker;
  • Customer name, address, email address, and phone number;
  • Invoice number, date, line items, quantities, prices, tax settings, and payment terms;
  • Bank or payment details you choose to print on an invoice, such as an account name, BSB, account number, routing number, bank name, account holder, IBAN, or SWIFT/BIC;
  • App settings, including language, currency, and theme;
  • PDF invoices saved inside the App;
  • A local record of whether a Google Play purchase for this App is active, so paid features can stay unlocked on the device.

The App does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or SMS messages. Choosing a logo uses the system photo picker for the image you select. That image is copied into the App's private storage and is not uploaded to us.

2. How that information is used

Information stored in the App is used only to provide the App's features:

  • Showing and editing your business profile and invoices;
  • Calculating totals and tax from the figures you enter;
  • Creating, saving, opening, and sharing PDF invoices;
  • Remembering your settings on this device;
  • Unlocking paid features after a purchase made through Google Play.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not show ads in the App, and the App does not include an analytics or advertising service that sends your invoice or customer information to us.

3. Google Play purchases

Optional paid features are sold through Google Play. The App offers a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription, and a one-time lifetime purchase. Google Play processes the payment.

We do not receive your card number, Google payment details, or bank login. Google may process your Google account, purchase, and related device information under Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service. The App receives the purchase status it needs to tell whether paid features are unlocked.

The App uses an internet connection for this Google Play billing check. You can manage or cancel a subscription in Google Play.

4. When you share or open a PDF

If you share or open an invoice PDF, Android hands that file to the app you choose, such as an email, messaging, or file app. The PDF can contain the business, customer, and bank details you put on the invoice.

That transfer happens because you started it. We do not receive a copy of the shared file. The app you choose handles the file under its own terms and privacy policy.

5. Backups and deletion

Android backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for this App's data. Your invoices and business records stay in the App's private storage on the device where you created them.

You can edit or delete invoices and business details inside the App. Uninstalling the App, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes the information stored by the App on that device. Because we do not keep a copy on our servers, we cannot restore it after it is deleted from the device.

Google may keep purchase records for the transaction under Google's own policies. Deleting the App does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel subscriptions in Google Play.

6. Security

Invoice and business information is kept in the App's private storage, which other apps on the device are not permitted to read. The security of your device, screen lock, and Google account also protects that information. No method of storage is completely secure.

7. Access and correction

You can view and correct the business, customer, and invoice information in the App, because it is stored on your device and is not held by us. For a question about information we do hold, such as a message you send us, contact us using the details below.

8. Children

EasyInvoice AU is a business tool. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Overseas processing

We do not upload your invoices or customer records to a service we operate, in Australia or overseas. Google may process purchase and Play-related information in Australia or in other countries under Google's privacy policy.

10. Complaints

If you have a question or complaint about this Privacy Policy, contact us first and we will look into it. If you are in Australia and you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at www.oaic.gov.au.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App or the applicable law changes. The effective date at the top of this page will change when we do. The current version is the page published at this address.
